RECOUNT TEXT

📌 Tujuan & Struktur

  • Purpose: Menceritakan pengalaman masa lalu (to retell past experiences).
  • Sequence: First, next, after that, finally.
  • Inference: Menyimpulkan informasi tersirat.
  • Reference: he, she, they, we, it.
Last Sunday, I visited my grandmother. First, I helped her cook. Next, we ate lunch. Finally, I went home.
NARRATIVE TEXT (FOLKTALE)

📌 Unsur & Moral

  • Tokoh, masalah, penyelesaian, pesan moral.
  • Simple Past: go → went, see → saw, help → helped.
A boy lost his friend's pencil. He told the truth and apologized. His friend forgave him.
Moral: We should be honest.
REPORT TEXT

📌 Social Function & Structure

  • Social function: To present general information about animals, plants, or other things.
  • General Classification: memperkenalkan & mengelompokkan objek.
  • Description: ciri fisik, habitat, makanan, kebiasaan, ancaman.
Komodo dragons are large reptiles from Indonesia. (General Classification)
They have strong legs and sharp claws. They eat meat. (Description)
SIMPLE PRESENT TENSE

📌 Aturan Verb

  • I / You / We / They → Verb 1 (They eat fruit.)
  • He / She / It → Verb 1 + s/es (It eats fruit.)
  • Plural noun → have / are (Orangutans have long arms.)
  • Singular noun → has / is (An orangutan has long arms.)
NUMERACY IN ENGLISH

📌 Operasi Hitung

  • altogether / in total = penjumlahan (+)
  • how many more / difference = pengurangan (-)
A wildlife team found 24 bird eggs on Monday and 17 eggs on Tuesday. How many eggs did they find altogether?
24 + 17 = 41 → They found 41 eggs altogether.
